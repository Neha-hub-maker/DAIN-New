Institutional Blueprint

About the DUNITE Achievement & Impact Network

DAIN is a centralized digital infrastructure designed to systematically identify, document, verify, and amplify the achievements, innovations, milestones, and impact stories originating from the Dhaka University community.

check_circle What DAIN Is

  • • PR & Media Distribution Engine: Transforms raw achievements into journalist-ready press releases.
  • • Institutional Archive: A permanent digital record of DU's intellectual and societal contributions.
  • • AI-Assisted Verification: Rigorous 3-stage validation ensuring only authentic, factual stories are syndicated.

cancel What DAIN Is Not

  • • Not a Standalone News Portal: DAIN syndicates stories to existing national media networks.
  • • Not a Paid PR Agency: We do not sell guaranteed coverage for promotional unverified content.
  • • Not a Self-Publishing Board: Stories must pass automated checks and editorial oversight before dispatch.
