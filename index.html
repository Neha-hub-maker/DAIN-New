The Strategic Rationale

Why Dhaka University Needs DAIN

Every year, Dhaka University students and faculty produce world-class publications, ventures, community campaigns, and patents. Yet a major portion remains undocumented or fails to reach mainstream media.

visibility_off

The Status Quo Challenge

Achievements remain trapped in isolated Facebook posts or departmental notice boards. Media outlets lack a structured, authentic source of verified success stories, resulting in lost recognition and under-documented institutional impact.

hub

The DAIN Solution

An institutionalized PR distribution engine that packages student and faculty stories into multi-format press releases, validates evidence through a 3-stage editorial workflow, and directly feeds verified stories to 100+ partner newsrooms.
